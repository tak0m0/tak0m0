# 💫 About Me:
Hi! I'm a high school student and software engineer based in Japan.
I started my career as a freelance developer in my third year of junior high school, building web applications and software solutions for real-world projects. Today, I also collaborate with startups on product development, working across a variety of technical challenges.
In 2024, I was recognized as a MITOU Junior Super Creator for developing an AI-powered English vocabulary learning application.
My current interests span AI-driven workflow understanding and visualization, UI/UX design, and audiovisual technologies for live events. I'm particularly interested in exploring how software can bridge the gap between complex technical systems and the people who use them.
What drives me is not simply building technology, but making complex systems and challenges more intuitive, accessible, and manageable for people.
I enjoy combining engineering and design to create products that improve real-world experiences and solve meaningful problems.
